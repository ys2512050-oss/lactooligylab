Miso

What is Miso?

Miso is a traditional Japanese fermented paste made from soybeans, salt, and koji. Depending on the recipe, rice or barley may also be used.

Fermentation

Miso develops over months or even years. During this time, enzymes and microorganisms create its deep umami flavor and rich aroma.
