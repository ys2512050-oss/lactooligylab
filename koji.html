Koji

What is Koji?

Koji is rice or other grains inoculated with the fungus Aspergillus oryzae. It plays a central role in Japanese fermentation and is used to produce miso, soy sauce, sake, and many other fermented foods.

Why is it important?

Koji produces powerful enzymes that break down starches and proteins into sugars and amino acids, creating rich flavors and improving fermentation.
